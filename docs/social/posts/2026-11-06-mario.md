### Catamaran Greece
Tema: Lokacija — Milos (Kikladi): Kleftiko i Sarakiniko
Slika: Kleftiko stijene s brodom u tirkiznoj vodi

**Facebook**
Some places are simply better from the water — Kleftiko on Milos is one of them. 🇬🇷
White volcanic cliffs, sea caves and arches, and water so clear your catamaran looks like it is floating in the air.
The old pirate hideout is best reached by boat — anchor, jump in and swim through the caves.
Then sail round to Sarakiniko, the moon-like white rock bay on the north coast.

Milos fits perfectly into a Cyclades week from Athens or Paros.

A catamaran makes it easy: big deck for the show, calm nights at anchor.
